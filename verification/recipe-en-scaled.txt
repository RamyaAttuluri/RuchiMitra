Andhra Chicken Curry
A vibrant, home-style Andhra curry layered with curry leaves, warming spices and a delicious fiery
finish.
Andhra · Non-Vegetarian
Medium
Preparation: 15 min
Cooking: 35 min
Total time: 50 min
Servings: 8
Ingredients
Chicken
1000 g
Onion
4 medium
Tomato
4 medium
Ginger garlic paste
4 tbsp
Oil
6 tbsp
Red chilli powder
3 tsp
Turmeric
1 tsp
Coriander powder
4 tsp
RuchiMitra · 1 / 3

Garam masala
2 tsp
Curry leaves
2 sprig
Salt
to taste
Water
1 cup
Cooking instructions
01 Get everything ready
Do not rinse raw chicken; pat dry with disposable towels, and chop onions and tomatoes. Wash
hands and utensils after handling raw poultry. Keep spices measured and ready.
5 min
02 Build the base
Heat oil in a deep pan over medium heat. Add curry leaves and onions; sauté until golden and
aromatic.
8 min
03 Add aromatics
Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for one minute. Stir in tomatoes and cook until soft and jammy.
6 min
04 Toast the spices
Add turmeric, red chilli powder, coriander powder, and salt. Stir for a minute, adding a splash of
water if needed.
2 min
05 Simmer the chicken
Add chicken and mix to coat. Add water, cover, and simmer gently for about 18–22 minutes,
stirring occasionally.
20 min
RuchiMitra · 2 / 3

06 Finish and serve
Check chicken reaches a safe internal temperature of 74°C / 165°F. Stir in garam masala, taste for
seasoning and serve with rice.
4 min
Cooking tip
For a milder version, reduce red chilli and finish with a spoon of yogurt.
